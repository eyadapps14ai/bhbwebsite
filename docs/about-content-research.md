# About Us — content notes

Prepared 9 October 2026. The page uses only the company facts and preliminary service areas supplied in the Phase 2 brief. No founding date, scale, awards, partnerships, registered-agent status or achievements are asserted.

## Official research

- Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism / Invest in Dubai: https://www.investindubai.gov.ae/en/business-setup/mainland-companies
  Business activity, legal structure and trade name are relevant starting points for mainland formation. This informed the activity-first wording; it does not verify BHB's licence or capabilities.
- UAE Ministry of Economy: https://www.moec.gov.ae/establishing-business-in-free-zones
  Requirements depend on the relevant free zone and activity. The page does not promise universal approval or one fixed setup process.
- Federal Tax Authority: https://tax.gov.ae/en/services/registration.of.tax.agents.aspx
  Registered tax-agent activity has specific requirements. BHB is not described as a registered tax agent, tax agency, auditor or regulated adviser. Tax wording invites a scope discussion and professional support rather than claiming representation.

All copy is original. The About page avoids detailed legal or tax claims and competitor copy.
